How it works

A hybrid retrieval and reranking pipeline grounds every open-source model response in a cited passage, then verifies the response before it's shown.

01

Screen the question

Images, patient identifiers, and time-critical questions are declined before any retrieval.

02

Retrieve & rerank

BM25 + BAAI/bge-m3 dense retrieval, reranked by BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3.

03

Generate

A written answer from NVIDIA Nemotron (via the DeepInfra API), or verbatim extractive quotes.

04

Verify & report

Every statement is checked against its source; failures are removed and logged.

Built to stay within FDA's non-device CDS criteria

Prompting an LLM to follow FDA's non-device clinical decision support criteria isn't reliable on its own — these checks run in code, on every statement, regardless of which generator produced it.

01

Every statement cites its source

Label section, DailyMed link, and label effective date — or a trust-checked web source.

02

Numbers must appear in the source

A dose, frequency, or threshold that isn't in the cited source is removed.

03

Directive statements are removed

"Give 500 mg" fails. "The label states the starting dose is 500 mg [S1]" passes.

04

Sources are ranked by trust

Approved healthcare sources are marked; anything else is clearly flagged, never hidden.

05

Abstains when unsure

When nothing can be verified against a source, TraceCDS says so instead of guessing.

06

Label tables are preserved

Dosing tables are converted to Markdown and quoted row by row with their column headers.

Q: How is exampledrug dosed for kidney function and eGFR?

  • According to the exampledrug label (Dosage and Administration (table), Table 1. Dosing by kidney function): "eGFR (mL/min): 45 or more, Recommendation: No adjustment; eGFR (mL/min): 30 to 44, Recommendation: Do not initiate" S1
  • According to the exampledrug label (Contraindications): "Exampledrug is contraindicated in patients with severe renal impairment (eGFR below 30 mL/min)." S2

Open retrieval, verifiable answers

Retrieval, source trust-checking, and the FDA non-device guardrails run in open-source code. Answers are written by NVIDIA Nemotron, an open-weight model served through the DeepInfra API; your question is sent there to generate the answer, and every statement it returns is still verified against the cited sources before you see it.
